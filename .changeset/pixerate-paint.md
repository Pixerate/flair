Add `pixerate-paint`, a path trail: a glowing neon ribbon, solid at its head, that breaks apart towards its tail into glowing square pixels that scatter, drift and twinkle. It takes `uUnit`, so it sizes to a paint brush, and it seeds its pixels by where they are, not by when trail points were laid, so a stroke painted with it keeps its pixels while they play. Its ribbon and squares are opaque, so it also holds up on light paper. It has a paint hint (`map: "path"`, live).
