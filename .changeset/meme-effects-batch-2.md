Six more "meme" effects: `needs-more-jpeg` (a picture recompressed step by step into 8 × 8 blocks, chroma bleed and mosquito noise, then snapping back clean), `triggered` (a red-tinted picture jittering fast, capped and without a strobe, with a banner bar of words), `vaporwave-grade` (a pink and cyan regrade with scanlines, a colour split, a striped sun over a rolling grid and a strip of glyphs), `galaxy-brain` (a glow from a head point climbing through four levels: halo, rays, nebula, starburst), `sad-mode` (a cool blue-grey drain with rain, a slow push-out and a heavy vignette) and `game-over-banner` (stuttering slow motion and a drain to grey, then serif words fading in on a dark band). Their words come from `textImage(...)` and are fitted to the ink, so any text fills its banner.
