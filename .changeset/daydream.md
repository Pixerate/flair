Add the `daydream` image filter (`cinematic` category): a picture seen as a remembered or imagined scene, for dream sequences, memories, flashbacks and thought bubbles. A diffusion glow that gently breathes, thin luminous wisps drifting across tinted by `uTint` (pale lavender by default), edges that dissolve into slowly shifting haze (`uFeather`), an optional shimmer of refraction ripple and sparse twinkles (`uShimmer`), a faded-colour control (`uFade`) and the usual backdrop controls, so with `uBackdropAlpha` 0 the picture floats over whatever is behind it. It loops seamlessly every 20 s, is tuned to stay legible as a small thumbnail, and ships with the `Daydream`, `Memory` (warm, faded), `Reverie` (stronger wisps) and `Floating` (transparent backdrop) presets, as Metal in `FlairEffects` (`Daydream`) and with preview screenshots.
