Add five webbing effects: `spider-web` (an orb web swaying in the breeze, beaded with dew), `cobweb-corners` (tattered cobwebs in the corners, transparent over the page), `web-shot` (a strand of silk shot to every click, bursting into a web splat), `plexus-web` (drifting points strung to their neighbours) and the `spider-web-loader` SVG loader. Find them together with `findEffects({ tags: ["web"] })`.
