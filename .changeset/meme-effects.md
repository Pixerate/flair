A new "meme" catalog category, browsed under Pictures, with a first batch of six effects: `impact-caption` (outlined Impact top and bottom text, the punchline popping on a beat later), `deal-with-it` (pixel sunglasses sliding down onto a pair of eyes), `deep-fried` (blown-out colour, oversharpening, JPEG blocks and glints at the eyes), `dramatic-zoom` (a stepped crash zoom onto a point), `laser-eyes` (beams firing from two eye points) and `to-be-continued` (a sepia freeze frame with an arrow sliding in). Points on the picture are 0–1 across the frame, y up, and default to the sample portrait's face.
