Six more `horror` effects, rounding out analog horror and the spectral and starting on creatures: `trail-cam` (an infrared trail camera's stills, date-stamped, with a figure closer in every shot), the SVG `emergency-broadcast` (a fictional "do not look outside" alert card switching on like an old set), `ectoplasm-wisps` (glowing trails spiralling in to the pointer), `spirit-orbs` (pale orbs drifting over a picture and darting off), `creeping-dark` (darkness with tendril edges closing in and recoiling from the pointer) and `shadow-hands` (the shadows of long-fingered hands reaching in from the edges).
