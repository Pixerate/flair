Add 16 glass and mirror effects to the catalog: `glass-text` (glass letters over a picture with a light beam that sweeps through and splits into a faint rainbow at the bevels), `chrome-text`, `glass-orb`, `glass-blocks`, `cracked-glass` (cracks at clicks), `condensation-wipe` (the pointer wipes fogged glass), `stained-glass`, `image-stained-glass`, `gemstone`, `prism-light`, `dichroic-glass`, `floor-reflection`, `water-reflection`, `infinity-mirror`, `funhouse-mirror` and the `transition-mirror-fold` transition. `ribbed-glass` and `shower-glass` gain a `uDispersion` control (off by default) and a "Prismatic" preset. Glass effects across categories are tagged `glass`, and mirrors `mirror` and `reflection`. The `SPECTRUM` GLSL helper (`spectrum(x)`, red to violet) is exported from `@pixerate/flair/catalog`.
