Add eight loaders to the `loader` category. Four are shaders drawn on clear, with a background a preset away: `liquid-loader` (a glass circle filling with sloshing liquid and bubbles), `glow-ring-spinner` (a glowing head sweeping round a ring with a breathing gradient tail), `metaball-loader` (gooey blobs chasing round and merging) and `flame-loader` (a progress bar burning along its length). The two that fill up loop over 6 s and have a still progress control. Four are animated SVGs that loop seamlessly: `hourglass`, `infinity-loader`, `orbit-loader` and `shape-morph-loader`.
