Text template export: `exportTextTemplate(scene)` exports a scene's words as an animated Bodymovin/Lottie title template for video editors, with editable text layers for titles, captions, element text and text effects, their fonts, boxes and word timings, and Lottie slots and fields to re-bind them. `exportScene(scene, format)` exports to any scene format by name, `flair scene export --format text-template` writes one, and agents get an `export_scene` tool.
