Two new light effects: `light-waves`, a glossy sheet of fine parallel ridges folding in slow waves with an iridescent sheen across the folds, and `light-strand-text`, tall letters filled with strands of light that follow the letters' edges, squeezing into corners and curving round counters, trail off the corners, and brighten and fade as light slides down them, with a few warm threads and a glow along the lower edges.
