Nine shader effects in the flat, loud style of a year-in-review story, each in the category it belongs to and tagged `recap`: `ribbon-bands`, `concentric-shapes` and `shape-tiles` (pattern), `stepped-gradient` (gradient), `echo-text` and `stretch-text` (text), `image-pixel-stretch` and `image-photocopy` (image, both video filters) and `transition-color-blocks` (transition). The four backgrounds have paint hints. All nine compile to Metal for the Swift package.
