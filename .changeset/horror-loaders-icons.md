The last of the horror additions, in the loader, interaction and icon categories, all tagged `horror`. Loaders: `coffin-loader` (a lid creaking open on eyes in the dark and slamming shut), `candle-burndown` (a candle burning down and blowing out), `ghost-loader` (a ghost floating round in a circle) and `skeleton-hand-loader` (bony fingers drumming on a table). Interactions: `cursor-follow-eyes` (eyes that follow the pointer, narrowing as it comes near; a transparent overlay), `mirror-mismatch` (a reflection that follows the pointer half a second late and sometimes does its own thing) and `haunted-hover` (a button that flinches away on hover). Icons: `jack-o-lantern`, `bat-flap` and `skull-chatter`.
