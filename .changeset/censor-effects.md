Add censor and redaction effects: `censor-pixelate`, `censor-blur` and `censor-scramble` hide a box or ellipse region of a picture or live video (or everything but it) behind mosaic blocks, a smooth mipmapped blur, or shuffled noisy blocks; `redaction-bars` draws rough marker bars over lines of a picture on a loop; and the `redact-box` annotation draws redaction bars over part of a screenshot or video and stamps REDACTED on top.
