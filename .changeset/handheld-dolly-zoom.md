Adds two camera effects. `camera-handheld` is a camera move a shot plays while it holds (`camera.during`): the camera drifts, sways and rolls round its framing as if held by hand, with `amount`, `speed`, `roll`, `footsteps`, `seed` and `motionBlur` controls and Subtle, Walking, Shaky cam and Found footage presets. It settles in from the framing it arrived at and zooms in just enough that the shake never shows past the framing's edges. `dolly-zoom` is a cinematic shader for pictures and video: the vertigo shot, the subject holding its size while the background behind it rushes in or falls away.
