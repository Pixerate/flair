More effects take a path. `border-beam` runs round any outline, `swoosh-underline` sweeps along any curve, and the pointer trails (`comet-trail`, `ribbon-trail`, `sparkle-trail`, `brush-trail`, `ghost-trail`, `light-painting`) follow a path while idle; each keeps its own shape until given one (`""` is no path). `signature` and `route` expose their path and fit their picture to it, and vector path controls take words (`{"text": …}`) in the editor too.
