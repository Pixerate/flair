Add `seed-head-burst`, a transparent light overlay for a hero moment. A fuzzy gold orb, like a dandelion seed head, comes into focus out of a cloud of golden dust: fine engraved rings and a fringe of spines sharpen. Then it bursts. Rays shoot out with glowing comet heads that ease to a stop and land as small nodes, some as purple orbs and the rest as cyan sparks, while the dust blows outward and away.

- Colours for the orb, its rim and spines, the dust, the rays, the comet heads and the nodes.
- The number of rays (4–32), orb size, ray reach, how much dust, glow, cycle length, and a still progress for a single frame.
- Presets: With background, Ember, Ice, Still burst and Many rays.
- It's also exported to the Metal/Swift package.
