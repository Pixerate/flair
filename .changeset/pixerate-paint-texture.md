"@pixerate/flair": minor
---

`pixerate-paint`'s ribbon now looks like a stroke of glossy paint rather than a smooth glow: bristle strands with darker grooves between them, the ribbon colour with the pixel colour twisting along one side, wet gloss streaks, a lit rim, and edges that waver. It is drawn in its own colours (opaque) under its glow. The texture measures along the stroke from where it began, so it stays put while the stroke grows, and fades as the ribbon breaks into pixels. A new `uTexture` control (default 1) sets how much of it shows; at 0 the ribbon is the smooth neon glow it was.
