Add a `horror` category (browsed under Pictures) with six effects, the first batch from the spooky-effects proposal: `vhs-tracking` (a cursed tape: warp, a rolling tracking band, head-switching tear, colour bleed and a camcorder's REC and clock), `night-vision-cam` (green phosphor night vision where pairs of eyes shine out of the dark), `signal-lost` (a feed that stutters, freezes and corrupts into a hidden picture, then cuts to static), `ghost-apparition` (a translucent figure fading in over a picture, with an optional double exposure), `eyes-in-the-dark` (glowing eyes that blink, watch the pointer and shut when it comes near) and the SVG `possessed-text` (letters writhing and floating out of line, then snapping back). Tags `analog-horror`, `spectral` and `creature` group them by kind; find them all with `findEffects({ category: "horror" })` or `catalog.search` with `category: "horror"`.
