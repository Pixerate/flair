Broadcast graphics and annotations: two new catalog categories of animated SVG effects in a 16:9, title-safe frame. `broadcast` has a lower third, a stacked lower third with a logo, a title card, a logo reveal, a logo sting, an end card, a countdown, a subscribe button, a news ticker, a scoreboard and a chapter marker; `annotation` has a hand-drawn circle and arrow, a callout, a bracket note, a scribble-out, step badges, a focus box, a product-demo cursor and a tooltip tour, all transparent to lay over video or screenshots. Each animates in, holds for its `hold` control and animates out, and takes a logo as SVG path data where it has one.
