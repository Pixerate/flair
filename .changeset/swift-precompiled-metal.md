Swift: documents whose GLSL was edited on the web now open and draw in `FlairEditorEffects` when they carry Metal compiled from that GLSL. `attachFlairMetal(document)` (`@pixerate/flair/node`) and `flair export metal-document <document.json> [output.json]` add it as `provider.metal` (the MSL, its uniform layout, the description for its controls and a SHA-256 of its GLSL). The Swift session shows and saves the GLSL with its Metal, drops the Metal when the GLSL is edited on the device (where it can't compile), and refuses Metal made from other GLSL, saying how to compile it. New in `FlairEffects`: `FlairRuntimeEffect`, `AnyFlairEffect(runtime:)` and `FlairRenderer.loadLibrary(msl:functionName:timeoutMs:)`, within the 2 s compile budget. The TypeScript adapter now keeps a document's `provider` data until its GLSL changes, opens GLSL given as the contract's `source`, and writes `source` and `sourceLanguage: "glsl"` in shader documents. Needs the editor's Swift package 0.42.0. `flair export metal` now writes `FlairRuntimeEffect.swift` and the `prepare` method `FlairRenderer` gained in the last release (its template had missed it); a test now keeps the templates and the Swift package's files the same.
