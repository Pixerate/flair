`relief-hex-tiles` and `relief-hex-ink` now visibly move: their tiles rise and fall in a wave that rolls across the cluster toward where it gathers, each tile a little out of step, instead of each tile drifting on its own (which read as still). The wave turns twice in the 8-second loop. `uVariation` ("Rise and fall") now sets how deep the wave goes, default 0.8.
