Eight more `horror` effects: the last of the creatures, a poltergeist, and the occult. `tentacles` (curling up out of the dark and reaching for the pointer), `bat-swarm` (bats wheeling across a dusk sky past the moon, scattering from the pointer), `passing-silhouette` (a tall figure walking once across a picture), the SVG `poltergeist` (an app's card, button, avatar and toggle lifting, wobbling and slamming back down), `summoning-circle` (a sigil drawing itself and flaring), the SVG text `rune-glow` (carved letters lighting up in turn, inline-capable), `cauldron` (a bubbling green brew under curling steam) and `candle-ring` (candles that gutter out all at once and relight round the ring). The occult ones are tagged `occult`.
