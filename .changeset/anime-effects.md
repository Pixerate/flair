A new `anime` category of effects, browsed under Pictures: `manga-tone` (a picture or video printed as a black-and-white manga page with screentones and hatching), `anime-sky` (a painted sky with cel-shaded cumulus and sun glare), `sakura-petals` (cherry blossom drifting down, as an overlay), `shoujo-sparkles` (a pastel backdrop of bubbles and sparkles), `gloom-lines` (despair lines hanging from the top of the frame), `menacing` (throbbing ゴゴゴ glyphs, any glyph you give it) and `eyecatch` (an anime ad-break title card). `focus-lines` and `anime-emotes` move here from `comic`; filter by `category: "anime"` (or the `manga` / `anime` tags) to find them.
