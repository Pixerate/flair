Add `hub-spoke-graph`, a data effect. A glowing hub lights up in a puff of dust, then spokes shoot out on comet heads to glossy pearl nodes, up to eight of them. Each node branches into small leaves and links to its neighbours, then sparks run out along the spokes as the hub pulses.

- `labels`: the spoke names, comma-separated.
- `hub`: the centre's label.
- `leaves`: leaves per spoke, 0 to 4.
- `backdrop`: turn it off to use the effect as an overlay.
- Presets: Light, Neon and Solar system.
