Add `ringed-orb`, a shader loader: a glossy, lit orb inside two rings turning opposite ways, one of fine radial dashes and one of drifting specks. It sits on a dark halo disk with a light sweeping round the disk's rim. It's transparent by default, so it can be dropped onto any card as a hero icon or a "thinking" indicator.

- Colours for the orb, its rim, the ring, accent glints and the disk.
- Disk opacity, size, the number of dashes, ring speed, rim-light speed and glow.
- Presets: With background, Gold, Cyan and No disk.
- It's also exported to the Metal/Swift package.
