Four card effects join the catalog: `card-flip` (a card turning over in perspective on hover, front picture to back), `scratch-card` (a coin scratching a ticket's foil away to reveal a picture, with a still progress control), `swipe-stack` (a dating-app stack flinging cards off under LIKE and NOPE stamps) and `pack-opening` (a foil booster pack tearing open and fanning out its cards, the rare one glowing).
