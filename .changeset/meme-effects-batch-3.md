Six more vector effects in the "meme" category: `stonks-chart` (a jagged line rocketing up, or crashing, to a fat arrow head with its value ticking up and a label), `error-cascade` (unbranded retro error dialogs popping up in a cascade or dragging a trail across the screen, with an editable title, message and button), `bonk` (a cartoon mallet swinging onto a point, the head squashing, an impact star and BONK popping), `hitmarker-montage` (hit markers landing at a list of points with a score floating off each and the frame jolting, the last in red), `reaction-rain` (live-stream reactions, any emoji or words, floating and wobbling up the side) and `wordart-text` (90s word art: a flowing rainbow fill, a skewed 3D extrude and a wave or arch). Each has an "Overlay" preset that drops the background for laying over a picture.
