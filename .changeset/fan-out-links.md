Add `fan-out-links`, a data effect: one source card sends a fan of curved links out to a column of up to ten targets, each drawn by a glowing comet head that lands on its label. The card's count ticks up as the links arrive, then sparks run along them. Targets go in `labels` (comma-separated) and the card's caption in `source`. `total` sets the count, and `backdrop` turns the background off for use as an overlay. It comes with Light, Neon and Team presets.
