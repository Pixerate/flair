Add five artist's-media image effects to the catalog: `image-pencil-sketch` (graphite or coloured pencil, with a blueprint preset), `image-watercolor` (layered washes with pooled edges and granulation), `image-crosshatch` (pen and ink as hatching, stippling or engraving, with woodcut and banknote presets), `image-charcoal` (smudgy charcoal, sanguine or chalkboard) and `sketch-reveal` (a picture drawn in pencil, shaded, then washed with colour, on a 6 s loop). The drawn looks take an optional line boil.
