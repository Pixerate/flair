Eight more `horror` effects: jump scares (tagged `shock`) and stylised gore (tagged `gore`). `scare-cut` (a sudden cut to a face with a zoom punch and a shake), `subliminal-flicker` (a single hidden frame now and then), `failing-light` (a fluorescent tube stuttering out over a picture) and `heartbeat-vignette` (a red pulse at the edges quickening as the pointer nears a target) are kept below seizure thresholds: none can be set to flash more than three times a second, and the heartbeat stops at 120 beats a minute. Then `blood-drip-border` (a transparent band of blood dripping from the top of the frame), the SVG `bleeding-text` (blood seeping through a title and dripping from it), `claw-slash` (claw marks tearing through a picture) and `blood-moon` (a crimson moon over dark hills).
